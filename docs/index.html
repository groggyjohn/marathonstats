Outline

This page presents various views of mass participation finishing times from runners of the London Marathon.

It has interactive plots, and the interactions works best with a computer which has a mouse or trackpad. The plots work on mobile devices, but some things work differently. On mobile you need to tap the plot to get point details displayed, and zoom needs to be done through '+' and '-' icons found on plots.

Marathon comments:

  • 2018: Hottest London marathon recorded so far (24.2°C)
  • 2020: No London mass participation event due to covid. Results are from the virtual marathon where registered runners completed their own route and uploaded their time via the official app.
  • From 2014 to 2020, age-range categories for over 70+ differed. From 2021 onwards the age categories settled, starting with 18-39, then 5-year intervals up to a maximum age cat of 80+.
